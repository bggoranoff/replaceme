# Canvas in a Cup

Level 3 of the barista practice environment.
Goal: Integrate espresso crema with microfoam and execute a centered 3-tier tulip latte art design.

## Procedure

1. Swirl espresso and polished milk to homogenize
2. Pour thin stream from 8cm height in circular motion to fill cup half-full without marking
3. Lower pitcher spout right against surface and increase flow rate to wrap white foam
4. Push forward, deposit second and third tier stacks while leveling cup
5. Lift pitcher 6cm and draw thin stream forward to complete center strike

## Success checks

- Verify sharp brown/white color contrast along cup rim
- Inspect symmetry across the central axis of the cup
- Ensure cup is filled to exactly 3mm below rim without spillage
- Confirm no bubble clusters on top design

## Game actions

Swirl espresso to break surface tension and blend crema, Tilt ceramic cup 45 degrees toward pitcher, Pour pencil-thin stream from 8cm height to establish liquid canvas base, Bring pitcher spout down to 1cm above surface to deposit white foam stacks, Cut through pattern with elevated thin stream from base to tip
