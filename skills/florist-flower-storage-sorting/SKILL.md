# Organizing Storage

Properly sorts incoming wholesale flower crates and botanical stems into species-specific microenvironments to extend vase life and prevent chilling or chemical damage. Key parameters include routing temperate blooms and foliage to refrigerated cold storage at 2°C–4°C, tropical stems to an ambient bench at 13°C–18°C, and Narcissus varieties to an isolated water vessel.

## Steps

1. Unpack arriving floral crates and inspect incoming species labels before bucket preparation.
2. Route temperate cut stems and foliage—including Dutch White Lilies (Lilium orientalis), Freedom Red Roses (Rosa), and Eucalyptus Foliage Bunches—directly into the Floral Cooler maintained between 2°C and 4°C.
3. Route chilling-sensitive tropical flowers—specifically Anthurium Blooms and Vanda Orchids—onto the Ambient Tropical Bench maintained between 13°C and 18°C.
4. Isolate all sap-bleeding Narcissus family varieties—specifically Paperwhite Jonquils (Narcissus tazetta) and Fresh-cut Golden Daffodils (Narcissus)—into dedicated Narcissus Isolation Vessels for at least 12 hours before considering any integration with other stems.
