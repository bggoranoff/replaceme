# Postharvest Cold & Ambient Sorting

Sort incoming postharvest flower and foliage shipments into appropriate environmental storage areas to maximize vase life and prevent chilling injury or toxic sap cross-contamination. ## Steps

1. Identify incoming temperate cut flowers and foliage (Eucalyptus Foliage Bunches, Dutch White Lilies [Lilium orientalis], and Freedom Red Roses [Rosa]) and store them immediately in the Floral Cooler set to 2°C–4°C.
2. Identify chilling-sensitive tropical blooms (Anthurium Blooms and Vanda Orchids) and place them on the Ambient Tropical Bench held at 13°C–18°C, never permitting exposure below 10°C.
3. Identify Narcissus species (Fresh-cut Golden Daffodils and Paperwhite Jonquils [Narcissus tazetta]) and isolate them in dedicated Narcissus Isolation Vessels filled with fresh water for a minimum of 12 hours before considering cross-flower arrangement.
4. Keep ambient tropicals away from refrigerated drafts and cold chamber shelving to avoid irreversible black chilling spots and petal drop.
5. Prevent water sharing between isolated Narcissus containers and buckets containing other flower types to stop toxic lycorine alkaloid sap transmission.
