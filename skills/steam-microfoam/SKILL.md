# Velvet Vortex

Level 2 of the barista practice environment.
Goal: Steam whole milk into glossy, silky microfoam at 60°C to 65°C without large surface bubbles.

## Procedure

1. Pour 200ml cold whole milk into cold 350ml stainless pitcher
2. Purge steam wand into damp cloth to clear condensation
3. Submerge tip slightly below surface, open valve full, stretch for 4 seconds
4. Sink wand tip deeper, maintain rolling vortex until thermometer reads 60°C, then shut off
5. Clean wand and tap pitcher on bench to collapse stray bubbles

## Success checks

- Confirm no high-pitched screeching during aeration
- Inspect milk surface for shiny wet-paint gloss
- Ensure milk temperature does not exceed 68°C scald threshold
- Verify steam wand is completely clean with no dried milk residue

## Game actions

Retrieve cold milk from fridge and fill pitcher to spout notch, Purge condensation from steam wand, Submerge steam tip 1cm and introduce air hiss for stretching, Tilt pitcher to create whirlpool vortex for integration, Shut off steam valve at target temp, wipe wand, and purge
